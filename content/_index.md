---
title: "Ultima Online Tools Archive"
---

A preservation archive of **Ultima Online (UO) development tools** for freeshard builders, scripters and game masters — map editors, art and MUL/UOP editors, animation tools, server emulators, GM utilities and classic Czech modding tutorials. Everything is free to download, rescued from community sites that no longer exist: **uo.wzk.cz** (2009–2017), **Manawydan / Eranova** by RadstaR (2004–2016), **ultima.cz** tutorials (2003–2014) and the **UO FreeShard Community Tool Box** (archive.org, 2019).
