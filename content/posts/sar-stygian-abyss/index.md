SAR (Stygian Abyss Re-Loader) is a utility for reloading and managing Stygian Abyss expansion content in the UO client. It can refresh or replace SA expansion data files without requiring a full client reinstall — useful when client files become corrupted or when switching between shards that use different versions of the expansion content.
