EasyUO is the most popular scripting platform for Ultima Online automation. It uses a custom scripting language designed specifically for UO, allowing players to write complex automation scripts for crafting, resource gathering, combat, and more. The archive includes multiple EasyUO versions (1.42, 1.5), the Codename Alexandria complete package (a modern EasyUO distribution with static libraries and redist packages), OpenEUO (the open-source successor), and the EasyUO MD documentation. At 130 MB this is the definitive EasyUO archive.
