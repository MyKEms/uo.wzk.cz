Terra Mystica Tool (TMTool) is a terrain editing utility for Ultima Online maps. It provides tools for modifying land tiles, elevation data, and terrain textures in UO map files. A simpler alternative to full map editors like CentrED or WorldForge for quick terrain adjustments.
