SpeedHack is a client-side speed modification tool for Ultima Online. It alters the game client's movement speed, allowing faster character movement than normally permitted. Commonly used on private servers where speed modifications are allowed by the shard rules. Use responsibly and only on servers that explicitly permit such tools.
