MultiList (Beta Release 5) is a viewer and browser for Ultima Online multi objects — structures composed of multiple tiles such as houses, castles, boats, and other complex in-game buildings. It allows previewing multi definitions from the multi.mul file, inspecting individual components, and exporting multi data. Useful for shard developers working with custom housing or structure designs.
